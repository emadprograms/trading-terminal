# Plan: 02.1-03 - Validation & Edge Cases

## Goal
Verify the synchronization logic under various market conditions.

## Context
Market transitions (Pre-market to Open, Open to Close) are the most likely times for REST vs WS discrepancies.

## Implementation Details
1. **Edge Case Handling**:
   - Timezone differences between REST (UTC usually) and WS timestamps.
   - Handling markets that are closed (no bridge needed).
2. **Stress Testing**:
   - Rapidly switching tickers.
   - Simulating high-latency bridge fetches.

## Verification Plan
- Manual testing during market hours.
- Automated regression tests.
