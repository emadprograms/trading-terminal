## Cross-Phase & Integration Issues
- **Alert Engine Disconnected**: `evaluatePrice` is never called by real market data.
- **Missing Asset Association**: Alerts do not track which ticker they belong to, meaning any price tick triggers alerts for all assets.
- **Hardcoded Initial Price**: The UI sets the baseline price to 100 instead of the real current price.
- **False Positive E2E**: The Playwright test manually invokes the engine to bypass the missing websocket integration.
